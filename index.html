Is this AI?

A free research tool that estimates whether a video was made or edited with AI.

How to use it on X

Reply to a post that has a video and mention @isthisaivideo. The account replies with an estimate.

Likely AILikely realCan't tell

What the answer means

The answer is a statistical estimate from a model that looks only at the video's frames and sound. It can be wrong in either direction. It is not a fact-check, and it says nothing about whether the people in a video did or said anything. Please don't rely on it alone.

Who runs it

isthisai is a small independent research project that builds and tests detectors for AI-generated video. We publish what we measure, including where the model fails.
